# VEOURES

**One body. Many hands.**

An unfamiliar life drifting alone in the dark. Watch long enough and it changes: limbs unfurl, skin contracts, new branches grow and old ones withdraw. No two moments need to look the same.

[Enter VEOURES](https://veoures.softaspen8.chatgpt.site/)

## How to play

Begin with **Observe**. There is nothing to win and no prescribed meaning. Let the creature move before deciding what to leave.

- **Grow Limb** — choose tissue inside the membrane for an internal fibre, or its edge for an outward appendage. Choose a variation or let the tissue decide.
- **Grow Eye** — place an eye in a clear area of living tissue. Its socket begins as a bud, opens gradually and develops its own blink and gaze.
- **Feed Mass** — offer volume to a region. It thickens the tissue and gently swells the skin, supporting nearby organs.

You can cancel any preview without spending a touch. Add your name before committing if you want a public credit; leaving it empty keeps the contribution anonymous.

## Four touches, then time

You receive **four accepted changes per personal day**. Your daily opening is assigned a random time, separate from other visitors. The next opening is shown in the controls. Watching, zooming, exploring and previewing are always available.

New tissue develops over **8–24 hours**, eyes over **24–48 hours**, and limbs over **36–72 hours**. Their anatomy remains. Older scars and tiny written traces still dissolve over four hours. Closing the page does not stop biological time.

## Move closer

Use +/−, the mouse wheel or two fingers to zoom. **Move view** lets you drag the scene; **Observe** returns to the whole creature. Keyboard users can select a tool, focus the creature, use arrows to choose a point, then Enter to preview. Inspect lists recent changes, status and a read-only time slider. Move through its history to see what the body was becoming, then return to the living present. Older writing can still be explored with Read traces.

Sound begins off. Turn it on only when you want to listen. Reduced-motion preferences are respected.

## From a living form to a collection

Every **414 accepted shared changes** records a different form in the collection. The creature continues becoming something else. These milestones are the material for a growing collection of original digital artworks, shaped collectively rather than chosen in advance.

Open **Inspect** to keep a form's portrait and its contribution credits. You can also keep the current unfinished form as a study. A portrait freezes one moment; the living creature keeps moving.

**Ranks** shows only contributors who choose to appear. You can order accepted changes by number, anatomical impact, evolutionary influence or recency, and inspect a contributor’s history. Names are self-chosen credits, not verified identities. Legacy names are kept as unverified credits.

Want to return as the same contributor on another browser? Open Your contributor identity inside Ranks, create and download a recovery key, and keep it private. Restoring it shares the same identity and four-touch allowance. You can hide your ranking again at any time.

Names and old written contributions are public. Only share what you want others to see.
