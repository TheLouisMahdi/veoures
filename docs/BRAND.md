## Meaning and voice

VEOURES names one living form shaped through many human contributions. The “ours” association is an intentional reading of the coined name, not a claim about linguistic origin. Meanings remain open to visitors. Use VEOURES in headings and product references, and lowercase `veoures` for paths and repository names.

Writing is brief, calm and concrete. Describe a trace, a body and a change. Explain whether changes stay in the browser or are shared. Avoid fictional missions, moral scoring, character lore or claims of live collaboration on the static demo.

Supporting copy stays lighter. Keep generous negative space and the living procedural organism as the main visual. Use local system fonts; no font download is required.

`assets/brand/social-card.png` is the 1200×630 public share/README image. It was rendered from this project's actual Canvas reducer and renderer, with native HTML typography. It is original project presentation, not a mockup of unavailable functionality or a replaced state-frame image. The canonical URL and Open Graph/Twitter metadata point to the public demo.
